## A · M7 (planta habitada)
Static top-down camera, no camera movement. Photorealistic humanized floor plan of a home office seen from directly above. A woman in light linen clothes enters through the door at the bottom right, walks slowly across the rug, picks up a book from the shelf and sits in the brown leather armchair. Soft daylight shadows move gently. Keep the floor plan, furniture and walls exactly as in the image. Calm, elegant, realistic motion.

## B · M9 (esboço de luz)
Same fixed camera, very slow push-in. Start: empty raw concrete room. Glowing thin white wireframe lines draw the furniture in the air — bookshelf, desk, office chair, armchair — line by line from the floor up. The lines turn warm gold, then fill with real materials: oak wood, leather, glass. Finish: the finished home office from the end frame, warm interior lights turning on. Architectural visualization, luxury, smooth morph.

## C1 · M8 parte 1 (do papel à estrutura)
Start: architectural floor plan on a desk. Glowing cyan neon lines trace the walls of the plan. The drawing lifts off the paper: wooden columns and roof beams rise out of the sheet, and the camera tilts up and flies forward into the real lakeside site at sunset, ending exactly on the end frame (timber structure by the lake). Seamless continuous morph, cinematic.

## C2 · M8 parte 2 (estrutura → casa pronta)
Same camera on the timber structure by the lake at sunset. Stone walls, large glass windows, dark roof and wooden deck build themselves rapidly over the frame; warm interior lights turn on one by one; reflections on the lake. Then a slow drone pull-back and rise revealing the house among araucaria trees. End on the finished lit house (end frame).

## D · M6 (estrutura explicada)
Start: house floor plan on a desk, 3/4 view. Concrete foundations appear on the walls of the plan, then columns rise from the paper, beams lock between them, the roof slab slides in. Glowing blue arrows flow down from the roof through the beams and columns to the foundations, showing the load path. Clean educational architectural animation, smooth continuous motion, slow orbit.
